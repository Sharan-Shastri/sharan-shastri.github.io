I am an **Electric Machines & Electromechanical Engineer** with 7+ years of industrial and academic experience in permanent-magnet motor design, motor-drive systems, electromagnetic FEA, controls, system validation, and engineering automation.

Currently, I work as an **e-Machines Systems Engineer** at **Jaguar Land Rover** (Bangalore, India), where I lead cross-functional digital twin and validation activities for automotive electric propulsion—spanning rotor topology optimization, electromagnetic/NVH evaluation, CAE-to-test correlation, and virtual validation using MATLAB/Simulink and dSPACE.

Previously, I was a **Motor Drives Engineer (Electromagnetic Design & Control)** in Research, Design & Development (RDD) at **Dyson** (Singapore), designing ultra-high-speed permanent-magnet motors ($>150\text{,000 RPM}$) and automating Python–ANSYS electromagnetic and flux-map workflows for closed-loop Simulink environments.

I completed my doctoral research (**Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering**) at the **Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi** on *Design and Control of Energy-Efficient Brushless Motors for Low Power Applications*, focusing on Halbach-array and consequent-pole PMBLDC topologies, custom motor drives, and precision reaction-torque measurement systems. Prior to IIT Delhi, I earned an **Integrated M.Tech + B.Tech** in Power and Energy Engineering from the **Central University of Karnataka**, graduating as a **Double Gold Medalist** (2017 & 2018).

### Core Areas of Focus

- **Electromagnetic Design & FEA**: PMSM & BLDC topologies, Halbach arrays, consequent-pole rotors, ultra-high-speed motors ($>150\text{k RPM}$), demagnetization & rotor-loss modeling (ANSYS Maxwell 2D/3D, Motor-CAD, Infolytica MagNet, FEMM).
- **Motor Drives, Controls & Digital Twins**: $d\text{-}q$ flux-map generation, controller–plant co-simulation, PWM/commutation strategies, and dSPACE / MATLAB / Simulink virtual validation.
- **Optimization & Automation**: Python–ANSYS automation, Design of Experiments (DOE), Response Surface Methodology (RSM), and topology optimization for torque-to-volume maximization.
- **Experimental Characterization**: Reaction-torque benches, NI cRIO & LabVIEW DAQ chains, sensor calibration, NVH/force evaluation, and test-to-FEA correlation.
