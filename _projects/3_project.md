### Overview

Completed as part of my doctoral research at **IIT Delhi** (*Design and Control of Energy-Efficient Brushless Motors for Low Power Applications*), this project developed low-cost, high-torque-density Permanent Magnet Brushless DC (PMBLDC) motors, custom electronic drives, and a precision experimental characterization test bench for domestic and industrial HVAC/ceiling-fan applications.

### Key Technical Contributions

1. **Novel Rotor Topologies (Granted Patents & IEEE Publications)**:
   - Developed **C-Type** {% cite shastri2021ctype_iemdc shastri2024patent_ctype %}, **Reduced-Rare-Earth Consequent-Pole** {% cite shastri2021consequent_ecce %}, and **Unequal Halbach-Array Assisted Consequent-Pole** {% cite shastri2024patent_halbach %} outer-rotor topologies.
   - Formulated a rapid composite Finite-Element (FE) method for determining the **Flux Concentration Factor** in Halbach-array PM rotors {% cite shastri2023rapid_jmmct %}.
2. **Control & Drive Development**:
   - Designed PMBLDC motor drive hardware and firmware, integrating commutation and control strategies into the electromagnetic design loop for system-level efficiency optimization {% cite shastri2020design_pedes %}.
3. **Reaction-Torque & Airflow Measurement System**:
   - Built an instrumented characterization bench integrating a reaction torque sensor, custom signal-conditioning electronics, **NI cRIO DAQ**, and **LabVIEW** {% cite shastri2024ceiling_tmi %}.
   - Performed end-to-end sensor calibration, uncertainty analysis, and test-to-FEA correlation across electromagnetic and aerodynamic operating points.
