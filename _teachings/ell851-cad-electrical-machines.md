## Role & Responsibilities

Served as **Course Teaching Assistant** across three offerings (**2019, 2020, and 2024**) of **ELL-851: Computer-Aided Design of Electrical Machines** at the **Indian Institute of Technology Delhi**.

## Core Topics Covered

- **Magnetic Equivalent Circuits (MEC)** & analytical sizing of induction, synchronous, and permanent-magnet machines (`PMSM` / `BLDC`).
- **2D & 3D Electromagnetic Finite Element Analysis (FEA)** using **ANSYS Maxwell** and **FEMM**:
  - Nonlinear $B\text{–}H$ material modeling and permanent-magnet operating point / demagnetization analysis.
  - Air-gap flux density harmonics, cogging torque, and torque ripple minimization.
  - Core loss (hysteresis, eddy-current, excess) and permanent-magnet eddy-current loss computation.
- **Design Optimization**: Parametric sweeps, sensitivity analysis, and multi-objective optimization for efficiency and torque density.
